Our Story

Craftsmanship You Can Build On.

LS Brickwork Ltd was built on a simple principle: every wall, every course and every finish should be done properly the first time. With over 15 years of hands-on experience, we’ve grown into a trusted name for brickwork, blockwork and full construction packages — working directly with homeowners, developers and main contractors to deliver builds that are structurally sound and beautifully finished.

From a single garden wall to a complete new-build home, we bring the same discipline, care and attention to detail to every project, whatever the size.

How We Work

Our Process

1

Foundations & Groundwork

Every project starts below ground. We lay accurate, specification-led foundations and blockwork to give your build a solid start.

2

Structural Brick & Blockwork

Walls rise course by course, with window and door openings framed precisely to specification as the structure takes shape.

3

Finishing & Detailing

Pointing, coping, lintels and decorative brickwork are finished by hand for a crisp, professional result.

4

Completed & Handover

We walk the finished job with you, making sure every detail meets our standard — and yours — before handover.
